# Part 1: Taking Ownership of an Estate Planning Matter

## 1. Issue identification

### Client and family data
1. Only one of two minor children is listed as a beneficiary in the intake questionnaire.
2. Spouse relationship needs confirmation: married clients, but no confirmed marriage date or property regime.
3. Guardian nomination for minor children may be incomplete.
4. Minor child support or special needs information is not documented.
5. Client update email from two weeks ago has not been incorporated into the drafts.

### Property and asset data
6. Title to the California home is unconfirmed.
7. Mortgage lender and payoff status are not recorded.
8. Property acquisition date and original vesting are unknown.
9. Community property vs. separate property analysis is incomplete.
10. Asset list for accounts, retirement, insurance, and business interests is incomplete.

### Document set
11. Revocable living trust draft exists but has no attorney review or approval.
12. Wills exist but may not match the trust terms.
13. Powers of attorney exist but have not been checked against the current client updates.
14. Beneficiary designations outside the will and trust may still point to old choices.
15. HIPAA authorizations may be missing.
16. Transfer deed for the home has not been prepared or reviewed.

### Process and communication
17. No client status update has been sent after two calls.
18. No single owner of the matter file is recorded.
19. No matter stage field is recorded.
20. No deadline field is recorded.
21. No record exists showing whether the attorney review will happen before Friday.

## 2. First-hour plan

### Minutes 0-15: Establish ownership and status
- Create or update the matter record.
- Add client names, matter number, matter type, stage, and owner.
- Set matter stage to "Attorney Scope Review / Intake Cleanup."
- Record the Friday target as the review-readiness date.
- Add a note that the supervising attorney is unavailable for most of the day.

### Minutes 15-30: Review the client update email
- Read the full email.
- Convert every change into a written list.
- Mark each item as incorporated, not incorporated, or needs attorney decision.
- Do not edit the draft documents until the list is complete.

### Minutes 30-45: Review the draft document set
- Compare every draft to the intake questionnaire.
- Compare every draft to the two-week client update.
- Compare beneficiary language against the list of both children.
- Compare property language against confirmed title status.
- Record exact discrepancies in a change log.

### Minutes 45-60: Send internal and client updates
- Send a status update to the clients.
- Send a concise attorney summary.
- Create follow-up tasks and calendar reminders.
- Schedule the next internal review checkpoint.

## 3. Independent action that can happen without the attorney

- Build the discrepancy log.
- Build the client update incorporation checklist.
- Confirm title and mortgage information by calling the client, title company, or county records.
- Confirm both children's full legal names and dates of birth.
- Confirm guardian preferences.
- Confirm beneficiary updates from the two-week email.
- Reconcile the intake data fields.
- Prepare a document version log.
- Prepare corrected draft versions for attorney review.
- Update the matter tracker.
- Send a non-legal status update to the clients.
- Confirm scheduling availability for signing.
- Prepare a funding and asset transfer checklist.
- Prepare a client document packet list.
- Research California estate planning forms and notarization requirements.
- Cross-check legal concepts and common doctrine with CourtListener where helpful.

## 4. Questions and decisions requiring attorney input

### Scope and strategy
1. Do both children receive equal distributions, or does one child receive a different distribution?
2. Should any child receive distributions at a different age or in trust rather than outright?
3. Should the trust include special-needs language or a subtrust?
4. Does the family want probate avoidance for all assets, or a limited trust plan?
5. Are there any disinheritance, unequal, or blended-family decisions?

### Property and funding
6. Should the home be transferred into the trust now or after execution?
7. How should the home title read after transfer?
8. Are there separate property contributions that need allocation?
9. Should a transfer-on-death deed be used as a backup?
10. How should retirement accounts and life insurance beneficiary designations be coordinated?

### Documents and fiduciaries
11. Who should serve as successor trustee and alternate?
12. Who should serve as executor, agent under financial power of attorney, and agent under healthcare power of attorney?
13. Should the agents be the same person or different people?
14. Should the attorney review the two-week email changes before they are inserted into final documents?
15. Should any document be held open for a future amendment or restatement?

### Risk and ethics
16. Are there signs of undue influence, family conflict, or capacity concerns?
17. Are both clients fully informed and voluntarily signing?
18. Does the matter require an engagement letter update or fee discussion?
19. Are there referral or specialist issues for business or foreign assets?
20. What should be communicated to the clients before attorney review is complete?

## 5. Client communication plan

### Immediate status email
Subject: Status update on your estate planning matter

Message structure:
- Thank them for their time and patience.
- Confirm that the file has been reviewed.
- Confirm that their two-week update has been located and is being incorporated into a discrepancy checklist.
- Confirm that the document set is being reconciled for both children.
- State that attorney review is the next internal step.
- State that title research on the home is underway.
- Provide the expected next update date.
- Provide a direct phone number and email for questions.

### Call response for clients who have called twice
- Apologize for the delay.
- Do not blame another paralegal.
- Confirm the specific next steps.
- Give a date for the next update.
- Ask whether any changes have occurred since their last email.
- Document every call in the matter file.

### Client update rule
Every client-facing fact should be one of:
- Done.
- In progress.
- Needs attorney review.
- Waiting on client.
- Waiting on third party.

## 6. Plan for Friday

### By Friday, aim to have
1. Complete discrepancy log.
2. Complete client update incorporation checklist.
3. Confirmed child and guardian data.
4. Confirmed or actively pending home title research.
5. Corrected draft set aligned with intake and client updates.
6. Attorney review request submitted with a clean summary.
7. Client status communication completed.
8. Document version log completed.
9. Signing readiness checklist completed.
10. Funding checklist prepared.

### Nothing falls through the cracks because
- Every item has an owner.
- Every item has a due date.
- Every item has a status.
- Every item has a blocker note if delayed.
- The matter tracker is reviewed daily.
- The attorney receives a one-page summary, not the full file.
- The client receives a same-day status update.
- A final Friday checklist is used before the matter is marked review-ready.
