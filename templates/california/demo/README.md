# California estate demo forms — research status

**Checked:** 2026-10-23; wills sections read from the official code 2026-11-15. **Use:** attorney-side product demonstration only.

The templates in `templates/california/demo/` are illustrative intake-shaped drafts. They are not firm forms, legal advice, approved instruments, or signing-ready documents. Counsel must verify current law and use firm-approved forms before any client use.

California Courts Self-Help, “Wills, estates, and advance care planning”: https://selfhelp.courts.ca.gov/wills-estates-probate/legal-documents (accessed 2026-10-23). This official page gives general descriptions of wills, trusts, advance health care directives, and financial powers of attorney; it does not verify the specific statutory execution rules for these templates.

## Probate Code sections read from the official text (2026-11-15)

Read in a normal browser from California Legislative Information, `https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PROB&sectionNum=<N>` (automated fetches get HTTP 403; a browser does not). Summaries, not quotations. These bear on the pour-over will; the trust, health care directive and power of attorney sections are still unverified.

| Section | What it says |
|---|---|
| 6110 | A will must be in writing and signed; at least two witnesses, present at the same time, must witness the signing or the testator's acknowledgment and understand the instrument is the testator's will. Amended 2008: a will not meeting the witness rule can still stand on clear and convincing evidence of intent. |
| 6111 | A will not meeting 6110 is valid as a holographic will if the signature and material provisions are in the testator's handwriting. |
| 6112 | Any competent person may witness. A gift to a subscribing witness creates a presumption of duress, menace, fraud or undue influence unless at least two other subscribing witnesses are disinterested. |
| 6300 | A will may give to the trustee of a trust identified in the will whose terms are in a written instrument executed before, with, or within 60 days after the will; the gift is not invalid because the trust is revocable or later amended. |
| 10400 | Short title: Independent Administration of Estates Act. |
| 21109 | A beneficiary who does not survive the transferor, or survive until a later time the instrument requires, does not take. |
| 21110 | The issue of a deceased beneficiary take in that beneficiary's place, unless the instrument shows a contrary intention; a survival requirement is a contrary intention. |
| 3920.5 | A will may continue a California Uniform Transfers to Minors Act custodianship only until a specified age no later than 25. |

Drafting consequences the pour-over will drafter should respect: no witness who takes under the will (6112); a survival period in a gift cuts off the beneficiary's issue unless the will names them as substitutes (21109–21110); the trust must be identified and its instrument signed within the 60-day window (6300); any custodianship age must be 25 or younger (3920.5).
