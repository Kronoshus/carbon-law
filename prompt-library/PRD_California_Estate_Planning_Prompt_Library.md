# PRD: California Estate Planning Prompt and Document Library

## 1. Purpose

Carbon Law needs working California estate planning documents and prompt templates for a solo or small-firm practice. This PRD scopes a prompt/document library for estate planning and asset transfer workflows in California.

## 2. Customer

- Solo or small-firm California estate planning attorney.
- Paralegal or legal assistant supporting 30 to 100 active matters.
- Clients who are married couples with minor children, homeowners, and account-based assets.

## 3. Two prongs

### Prong 1: Most useful
- Revocable living trust
- Pour-over will
- Durable financial power of attorney
- Advance healthcare directive
- HIPAA authorization
- Guardian nomination
- Trust funding instructions
- Beneficiary designation tracking
- Estate planning questionnaire

### Prong 2: Distinctively California
- California-specific property characterizations
- Community property and separate property issues
- California deed transfer into trust
- California notary and witness requirements
- California conservatorship avoidance
- California beneficiary rules and probate threshold considerations

## 4. Practice-area taxonomy files
1. Estate Planning Intake and Client Data
2. Revocable Living Trust Drafting
3. Wills and Pour-Over Wills
4. Powers of Attorney
5. Healthcare Directives and HIPAA
6. Minor Children and Guardian Nominations
7. Real Property Transfer and Deed
8. Trust Funding and Asset Coordination
9. Beneficiary Designations
10. Signing and Execution Readiness
11. Post-Execution Funding Follow-Up
12. Matter Status and Client Communication

## 5. Prompt count targets
- 5 to 15 prompts per taxonomy file.
- First release total target: 100 prompts.
- Every prompt must contain an agent noun in its title.
- Every prompt must include user inputs, response quality protocol, and a test run.

## 6. Boundary rules
- Intake prompts do not draft final legal advice.
- Trust drafting prompts do not replace attorney review.
- Deed-related prompts flag recorder and title requirements.
- Healthcare prompts avoid medical advice.
- Status-update prompts avoid legal advice in client communications.

## 7. Output format
Each prompt must include:
- Title with -er/-or agent noun
- Description
- Expert role
- User inputs
- Uploads
- Response quality protocol
- Test run

## 8. Verification
- Cross-check citations and concepts with CourtListener and California Legislative Information.
- Every legal statement must carry appropriate caution and attorney-review boundary.
