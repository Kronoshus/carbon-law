## (a) Google Forms build sheet

### Settings
- **Settings → Responses:** Collect email addresses: **Do not collect**. Limit to 1 response: **off** (so nobody has to sign in).
  Allow response editing: off.
- **Settings → Presentation:** Show progress bar: on. Shuffle question order: off.
- **Settings → Defaults:** Make questions required by default: **off**. No question is required.
- **Responses tab:** link to a new Google Sheet in the firm's account. The firm sends the answers to Kevin.
- Send the form as a link by text or email. Nobody needs to sign in.

### Form title
Carbon Law Group: staff questionnaire / Cuestionario para el personal

### Form description
English: About 15 minutes. For attorney Pankaj Raval and staff who talk with estate-planning clients. It helps us check the
Aprende app against what the office sees. Answer from memory; rough counts are fine; "I don't know" is a useful answer.
Please do not write client names or details that identify a client. No email address is collected.

Español: Unos 15 minutos. Para el abogado Pankaj Raval y el personal que habla con clientes de testamentos y fideicomisos.
Nos ayuda a comparar la aplicación Aprende con lo que ve la oficina. Conteste de memoria; los números aproximados están
bien; "No sé" también es una respuesta útil. Por favor no escriba nombres de clientes ni datos que identifiquen a un
cliente. No se pide su correo electrónico.

### Section 1: Language / Idioma
**0. Language / Idioma**  
Type: Multiple choice, with "Go to section based on answer"
- English → go to Section 2
- Español → go to Section 3

### Section 2: English
Section description: Answer from memory. No client names. Group headings can be added as Title blocks:
A. Who comes in (1–3) · B. Before they hire the office (4–6) · C. During the work (7–11) · D. Signing and after (12–14) ·
E. The app (15–18).
After Section 2: **Submit form**.

**1. Of your estate-planning clients in the last 12 months, about what share preferred Spanish? (percent)**  
Type: Short answer  
Setting: Response validation: Number, between 0 and 100.

**2. How do most of them find the office? Check all that apply.**  
Type: Checkboxes
- Referral from a client
- Family
- Radio or TV
- Web search
- Social media
- Community event
- Other (with text box)

**3. What usually makes them call now? Pick the top three.**  
Type: Checkboxes
- Illness or hospital stay
- A death in the family
- Buying a home
- A new child or grandchild
- Worry about who would care for their children
- Travel
- Retirement
- A family member pushed them
- Other (with text box)
Setting: Response validation: Select at most 3.

**4. Of 10 people who ask about a will or trust, about how many sign an engagement letter?**  
Type: Linear scale  
Setting: Scale 0 to 10. Labels: 0 = none / ninguna, 10 = all / todas.

**5. Why do people stop before they hire the office? Rank the top five (1 = most common).**  
Type: Multiple-choice grid
- Price
- "I'll do it later"
- The family disagrees
- They don't trust lawyers or fear fraud
- They think they don't own enough
- They found a cheaper option (online form, notario)
- Language
- Worry about how their personal information will be used
Setting: Rows = the reasons above. Columns = 1, 2, 3, 4, 5. Turn on "Limit to one response per column". Leave "Require a response in each row" off. Add a Short answer right after it: "5b. Other reason (optional)" / "5b. Otra razón (opcional)".

**6. What do people most often believe that is not true? (For example: "my spouse gets everything anyway", "a will avoids probate".) List up to five.**  
Type: Paragraph

**7. Which intake topics take the longest or need the most follow-up? Check all that apply.**  
Type: Checkboxes
- Assets and titles
- Beneficiaries
- Guardians for children
- Choosing an executor or trustee
- Health care wishes
- Powers of attorney
- Contact details
- Other (with text box)

**8a. How many meetings or calls does a typical will-only client need?**  
Type: Short answer  
Setting: Response validation: Number.

**8b. How many meetings or calls does a typical will-and-trust client need?**  
Type: Short answer  
Setting: Response validation: Number.

**9. About how many minutes of attorney time per client go to explaining basics (what a trust is, what probate is)?**  
Type: Short answer  
Setting: Response validation: Number.

**10. What do clients most often get wrong or forget to bring?**  
Type: Paragraph

**11a. Of 10 clients, how many ask for changes after they see the drafts?**  
Type: Linear scale  
Setting: Scale 0 to 10.

**11b. What kinds of changes are most common?**  
Type: Paragraph

**12. What goes wrong around signing day? Check all that apply.**  
Type: Checkboxes
- Witnesses
- Notary
- No-shows
- Missing ID
- Family members who come along
- Other (with text box)

**13a. Of 10 trust clients, how many finish moving their home and accounts into the trust?**  
Type: Linear scale  
Setting: Scale 0 to 10.

**13b. What stops the rest?**  
Type: Paragraph

**14. Of 10 clients, how many come back later to update their plan?**  
Type: Linear scale  
Setting: Scale 0 to 10.

**15. Which would save you the most time? Rank 1–5 (1 = most time saved).**  
Type: Multiple-choice grid
- Clients understand the basics before they meet the attorney
- Intake answers collected in advance
- Fewer missing documents at signing
- Fewer reminder calls
- More clients finish moving assets into the trust
Setting: Rows = the five items. Columns = 1, 2, 3, 4, 5. Turn on "Limit to one response per column".

**16. What worries you about clients learning from an app with a talking character?**  
Type: Paragraph

**17. Is there anything a character must never say, beyond legal advice, fees and promises?**  
Type: Paragraph

Type: Paragraph
