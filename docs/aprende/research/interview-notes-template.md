# Interview notes template (one copy per interview)

Fill in one copy the same day as the interview. Write in the language of the interview. Section numbers match
`client-interview-guide.md`.

**Never write here:** name, address, phone, email, employer, exact age, what their assets are worth, family members' names,
or family details that could identify someone. If the person talked about where they were born or their status in this
country, leave it out.
