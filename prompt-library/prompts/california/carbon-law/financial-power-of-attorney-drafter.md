## California research anchors
Probate Code §§ 4121–4122 and 4401 are research anchors for execution sufficiency and the statutory form. Counsel must verify current law and select/use the firm's approved form.

## Drafting instructions
- Organize only provided principal identity, agent and alternate choices, expressly selected scope, gifting authority, real-property authority, and any stated effective-time choice.
- Never infer immediate versus springing effectiveness, durability, special powers, agent compensation, successor order, guardianship/limitations, or statutory-form elections.
- Treat general descriptions like “everything” as ambiguous; preserve them verbatim as a question rather than translating into legal powers.
- Mark missing/ambiguous terms `[ATTORNEY TO COMPLETE — ...]` and list them in `Attorney review / missing information`.
- Do not output signature, witness, notarial acknowledgment, or execution instructions. Do not claim it grants valid authority.
