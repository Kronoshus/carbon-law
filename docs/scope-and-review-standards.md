# Scope and Review Turnaround Standards

## Scope standards

### Core estate planning scope
All matters include intake, conflicts, attorney scope review, trust, will, financial power of attorney, healthcare directive, guardian nomination, and signing preparation unless otherwise agreed.

### Default exclusions unless engagement letter adds them
- Probate administration
- Trust administration after death
- Tax planning beyond standard provisions
- Business succession planning
- Foreign assets
- Special needs or pooled trust design
- Real property title insurance review
- Post-signing legal changes after execution

### Attorney-required decisions
- Distribution amounts and timing
- Guardian and fiduciary selection
- Special-needs provisions
- Business or foreign assets
- Disinheritance or unequal shares
- Advanced tax or trust structuring

## Review turnaround standards

- New matter scope review: 2 business days.
- Paralegal discrepancy log to attorney summary: 1 business day.
- Attorney review of corrected draft packet: 3 business days.
- Client requested revisions: 2 business days after attorney approval.
- Final execution packet: 1 business day.
- Post-signing funding follow-up: 5 business days.
- Urgent client change request: same day acknowledgment and next-day attorney triage.
